Wendell's Quest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Wendell's Quest ("the game") is made for players of all ages, including children. We collect as little as possible.

What we collect

When Post scores online is ON (the default), finishing a campaign or reaching game over and entering initials sends only the data below to the online Top 20 board:

DataExampleWhy
3-letter initials typed by the playerWENShown next to the score on the public Top 20
Score, campaign and number of levels completed4200, Beginner, 10To rank the Top 20

We do not collect names, email addresses, accounts, device identifiers, advertising IDs, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. Scores are not linked to a person or a device.

Turning it off

Parents or players can turn off Settings > Post scores online. When it is OFF, the game sends nothing to our server: it posts no scores, downloads no scores and keeps no retry queue (any scores waiting to be sent are deleted). Scores are still kept on this device. The setting is saved and is not changed by Reset progress.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad networks
  • No analytics or third-party tracking SDKs
  • No in-app purchases
  • No chat or messaging between players
  • No links that leave the game

Where it's stored

Top 20 entries are stored with our database provider, Supabase, which hosts them for us and does not use them for its own purposes. Progress, stars and the "This Device" scores are saved only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Initials are checked against a blocked-word list. Entries that slip through can be removed when we find them or on request.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The initials and score cannot identify a child. Parents can ask us to remove any Top 20 entry by contacting us below.

Retention

Top 20 entries stay up until they drop out of the list or we remove them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
